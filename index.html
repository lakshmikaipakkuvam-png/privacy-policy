Privacy Policy — Zyvo Content Assistant Last updated: October 18, 2026 What this app does The Zyvo Content Assistant connects your Pinterest account to a personal AI assistant. With your permission, it reads your own boards and pins to use as creative references when designing content. Data we access Your boards: names, descriptions, and privacy status Your pins: titles, descriptions, and images We access only the authenticated user's own boards and pins. We do not access any other Pinterest user's data. Data we do not collect or share We do not publish, share, or sell your Pinterest data to any third party. We do not create, modify, or delete pins or boards on your behalf. How data is used Pinterest data is used solely to provide creative references inside your private assistant session. Access tokens are stored securely and used only to make API requests on your behalf. Data retention and revocation Access tokens are kept only while the integration is active. You can revoke access at any time from your Pinterest settings (Settings → Apps), which immediately stops all data access. Contact Questions about this policy: evidratech@gmail.com
